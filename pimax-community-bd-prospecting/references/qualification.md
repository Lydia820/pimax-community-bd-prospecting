# Qualification and Scoring

## Hard acceptance gates

An accepted prospect must satisfy all of these:

1. Operates a relevant audience, community, event, league, website, platform, or creator channel.
2. Has observable relevance to PCVR, high-end VR, VR games/VRChat, sim racing/iRacing, or flight simulation/virtual aviation.
3. Shows current activity: normally within 12 months; up to 24 months only with strong ongoing infrastructure or audience evidence.
4. Publishes a direct email or usable Discord invite/username attributable to the operator.
5. Has a plausible cooperation route and a concrete Pimax-fit rationale.
6. Is not an exact/normalized duplicate of a prior accepted batch.
7. Has no confirmed existing Pimax relationship unless the task explicitly requests existing-partner expansion.

Contact forms, social handles without a DM route, redacted emails, guessed addresses, expired invites, and generic website placeholders do not pass gate 4.

## Score out of 100

- Pimax audience fit: 0–30
- Recency and operational activity: 0–20
- Explicit cooperation/sponsor/partner signal: 0–20
- Direct-contact quality: 0–15
- Audience scale or strategic leverage: 0–10
- Country/language confidence: 0–5

Classification:

- **A:** 80–100 and every hard gate passes.
- **B:** 65–79 and every hard gate passes.
- **Pending:** potentially useful but one gate or material fact needs verification.
- **Excluded:** duplicate, existing Pimax relationship, irrelevant, unsafe/private contact, clearly stale, or fake/placeholder contact.

Do not promote a pending candidate merely to increase the accepted count.

## Evidence confidence

- **High:** official current page or official community directory supports the contact and operating facts.
- **Medium:** an attributable operator post supports the facts, but one operational detail remains indirect.
- **Low:** snippets, aggregators, or unverified third-party mentions. Low-confidence candidates do not enter the main sheet.

## Mailbox and CRM wording

If a connected mailbox search finds nothing, record: `当前连接邮箱未发现往来；CRM待核`. Do not rewrite this as “未建联”. If no CRM export or CRM connector was inspected, always use `crm_status=unchecked`.
