# Sourcing Playbook

Use several public lanes in each run. Prefer primary/official pages and recent posts.

## Sim racing and iRacing

Search for league, team, tournament, broadcast, academy, tool, and community operators. Useful signals include current seasons, weekly race calendars, sponsor packages, partner pages, title sponsors, broadcast inventory, hardware prizes, and public league Discords.

Query patterns:

- `sim racing league sponsor partner Discord 2026`
- `iRacing league sponsorship package email`
- `sim racing community partner with us`
- `site:reddit.com/r/iRacing sponsorship Discord league`

## Flight simulation and virtual aviation

Search virtual airlines, MSFS/X-Plane communities, developer groups, flight-sim media, event organizers, and platform partner programs. Favor active pilot statistics, current ACARS/releases, event calendars, staff recruitment, creator programs, and explicit partnership pages.

Query patterns:

- `virtual airline partnership Discord MSFS`
- `flight simulator community sponsor email`
- `MSFS partner program creator community`
- `site:reddit.com/r/flightsim virtual airline Discord 2026`

## VR, VRChat, and VR esports

Search official game communities, VR esports leagues, tournament operators, VRChat event groups, fitness/sports communities, XR media, and collaboration-ready creators. Favor current event seasons, verified community sizes, creator programs, sponsor pages, and direct organizer contacts.

Query patterns:

- `VR esports sponsor Discord partner`
- `VRChat community partnership Discord`
- `VR tournament sponsor contact email`
- `VR creator business contact PCVR 2026`

## Source priority

1. Official contact/partner/sponsor page.
2. Official Discord directory or invite linked from the canonical site.
3. Recent official recruitment/event post with direct contact.
4. Current creator channel description with business email and relevant recent content.
5. Reddit/forum post only when the poster clearly represents the operator and the contact is explicit.

Search results may discover candidates, but accepted rows must retain the canonical source or a directly attributable operator post.

## Relationship and duplicate checks

Search the candidate name/domain together with `Pimax`, and inspect prior workbooks. Public Pimax event, affiliate, review, sponsorship, or partner evidence is sufficient to remove the candidate from the new-prospect main sheet pending internal review.
